Requiem for a belt
Interactive visualization tool
Instructions:
Left click and drag: rotate · Mouse wheel: zoom · Right click and drag: pan · Press [R] key: reset camera view · Press [T] key: go back to the present · Drag slider handle: move across time
Acknowledgements:
This tool was created by Michelangelo Pantaleoni with the use of research-based AI tools for the paper “Requiem for a belt: A spatial and kinematical reinterpretation of Gould's Belt in light of Gaia” .
